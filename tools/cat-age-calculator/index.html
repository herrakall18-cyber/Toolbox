How to convert cat years to human years

Many pet owners ask, “how old is my cat in human years?” — but the answer isn’t a simple multiply by seven. Cats mature rapidly in their first two years: a 1‑year‑old cat equals about 15 human years, and a 2‑year‑old cat equals about 24 human years. After that, each additional cat year adds roughly 4 human years. This cat age calculator uses the widely accepted veterinary formula to give you a reliable conversion.

Knowing your cat’s human‑equivalent age helps you provide the right care. A “senior” cat (around 11 human years, or 60+ in cat years) may need different food, more frequent vet check‑ups, and adjustments to their environment. Whether you have a playful kitten or a wise old companion, this cat years to human years tool makes it easy to understand their life stage.

How it works: If your cat is 2 years or younger, we use a special early‑life curve. For cats older than 2, the formula is: 24 human years + (cat years − 2) × 4. For kittens less than 1 year old, we adjust using months. The result shows both the human years and the corresponding life stage (kitten, young adult, adult, senior, or geriatric) — so you can better support your feline friend.
