Many students underestimate the impact of a final exam. With this grade needed on final tool, you can see whether a strong performance can pull you up — or whether you can relax if you’ve already secured your goal. The formula is simple: (Desired Overall – (Current × (1 – Weight))) / Weight. It works for any grading scale where the final is a percentage of the total course. Use it before your next exam to remove the guesswork.
