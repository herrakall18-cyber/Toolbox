Why use an LED current limiting resistor?

LEDs are current‑driven components: a tiny change in voltage can make them draw excessive current. Without a resistor, even a 0.5V over‑supply can lead to overheating and failure. This LED resistor calculator uses the basic formula R = (Vsupply – Vforward) / I to determine the exact current limiting resistor for LED operation. It also gives you the minimum power rating, so your resistor won't overheat either.

Simply enter your supply voltage (e.g., 5V from USB or 12V strip), the LED forward voltage (typically 2.0–3.6V depending on color and type), and the desired current (usually 10–20 mA for indicator LEDs). The calculator instantly outputs the nearest resistor value and the safe wattage rating. You can then pick the closest standard resistor (e.g., 220 Ω, 470 Ω, 1kΩ).

Use this led resistor value calculator whenever you prototype or design circuits. It saves time, prevents blind guessing, and reduces the risk of frying your LED. The calculator handles any DC supply, as long as supply voltage is higher than forward voltage. For multiple LEDs, just recalculate with your specific wiring (series or parallel).
